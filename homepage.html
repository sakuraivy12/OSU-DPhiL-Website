We are the Phi Chapter of Delta Phi Lambda, Sorority, Inc at The Ohio State University!

Chartered on March 31st, 2019, at The Ohio State University, our chapter is committed to fostering an inclusive and empowering community for Dedicated Female Leaders. We strive to provide a “home away from home” for women who share our commitment to upholding the seven core virtues of Loyalty, Honesty, Respect, Dedication, Integrity, Discipline, and Academic Excellence. With a focus on advocating for Asian awareness and driving positive change, we are united in our mission to create a lasting impact through Everlasting Sisterhood.
